Privacy Policy

Effective: May 5, 2026 · Last updated: May 5, 2026

We respect your privacy. This page explains, in plain language, what information we collect when you use the diagnostic, how we use it, and the rights you have over it.

Julianna Systems (referred to as we, us, or our) operates the Hidden Cost of Turnover and Burnout diagnostic. Julianna Systems is the B2B advisory brand of Julianna Harry, a registered psychotherapist operating Grey Couch Counselling in Atlantic Canada.

This policy applies specifically to the diagnostic tool. If you become a Julianna Systems client, additional terms and privacy practices may apply to that engagement and will be shared with you at that time.

1. Information we collect

Information you provide directly

  • Your name and work email address, given when you unlock your results.
  • Your diagnostic responses: industry, role, workforce numbers, average salary, time to fill a role, sick days, manager hours, and selected burnout signals.
  • Your consent to these terms and to receive marketing communications, recorded with a timestamp.

Information stored on your device

  • Your name, email, and consent record, so you do not have to re-enter on return visits.
  • Your most recent calculator inputs, so you can pick up where you left off.

This information lives in your browser's local storage. It does not travel to us until you actively submit the form.

Information collected automatically by hosting infrastructure

  • Standard request data when you load the page, including browser type, time of access, and approximate region. This is normal web hosting telemetry.

What we do not collect

  • We do not require or collect phone numbers, postal addresses, or payment information through this tool.
  • We do not use third-party advertising trackers, behavioral profiling tools, or social media pixels on this page.

2. How we use your information

We use the information you provide to:

  • Send your full diagnostic results to your email.
  • Follow up about the Burnout Audit, retention programs, workshops, and other Julianna Systems offerings.
  • Personalize your results experience.
  • Improve the tool over time, in aggregate (we look at patterns, not individuals).

3. Where your information is stored

To deliver your results and follow up, we use the following service providers. Each is bound by their own privacy practices:

  • Web3Forms receives your form submission and forwards it to Julianna's inbox. (privacy policy)
  • Vercel hosts this website and may log standard request data. (privacy policy)
  • Google Fonts serves the typefaces used on this page. May receive your IP address as part of a font request. (privacy policy)

If we add additional tools (such as an email-marketing platform or a customer database), we will update this policy and notify you on the page itself.

4. Cookies and local storage

This tool does not set third-party advertising cookies. It does store the items listed in section 1 ("Information stored on your device") in your browser's local storage. You can clear this at any time by clearing your browser's local storage for this site.

5. Your privacy rights

Under Canada's Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA) and equivalent provincial privacy legislation, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you.
  • Correct any inaccuracies in that information.
  • Withdraw your consent and request deletion of your information.
  • Receive a portable copy of the information you have provided.
  • File a complaint with the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.

To exercise any of these rights, email julianna@greycouchcounselling.com. We will acknowledge your request within 7 days and respond substantively within 30 days.

6. Marketing communications

When you check the consent box on the unlock screen, you give express consent under Canada's Anti-Spam Legislation (CASL) to receive electronic communications from Julianna Systems related to your diagnostic, the Burnout Audit, and adjacent offerings.

Every marketing email we send will contain an unsubscribe link. You can also unsubscribe at any time by emailing julianna@greycouchcounselling.com with the subject "Unsubscribe."

7. Data retention

We retain the personal information you provide for as long as needed to deliver your results, follow up appropriately, and meet our legal obligations. If you withdraw consent or request deletion, we will remove your information from our active systems within 30 days, except where retention is required by law.

8. Children's privacy

This tool is intended for adult business leaders and is not directed at anyone under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe we have collected information from a child, please contact us and we will delete it.

9. International transfers

Some of our service providers (notably Vercel and Web3Forms) may store or process information on servers located outside Canada, including in the United States. By using this tool, you consent to your information being processed in those jurisdictions, subject to safeguards required by Canadian privacy law.

10. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect the information you share with us. Submission to our form is transmitted over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection. No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure, however, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected in the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of the tool after a change indicates acceptance of the updated policy.
