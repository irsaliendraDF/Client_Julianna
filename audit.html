Letter of Consent & Confidentiality Agreement

Welcome to the Julianna Systems Organizational Health Assessment. This platform has been commissioned by your employer to gather candid, anonymous feedback about workplace experience, culture, and well-being.

Your identity is fully protected. Julianna Systems serves as an independent third party and will not share, disclose, or transmit any identifying information about you, including your unique identifier, to your employer or any affiliated parties under any circumstances.

All survey responses, ratings, and voice transcripts are anonymized before any analysis is performed or shared. Reports provided to your organization contain only aggregated data organized by department, role level, tenure, and demographic indicators, never by individual.

The demographic information you provide (age range, gender identity, department, role level, tenure) is collected solely for the purpose of identifying systemic patterns. For example, whether employees of a particular gender or in a particular department are experiencing the workplace differently. This data helps surface issues that might otherwise remain invisible.

⚠ Mandatory Disclosure Exception: If at any point during this assessment you disclose information indicating illegal activity (including but not limited to fraud, abuse, harassment, or criminal behaviour), Julianna Systems is obligated to report this information to the appropriate authorities. This is the sole exception to our confidentiality commitment.

Your participation is entirely voluntary. You may stop at any time. By proceeding, you confirm that you are completing this assessment of your own free will and understand how your responses will be used.

Questions? Contact: privacy@juliannasystems.com
